What your agent does each month

MABLE calls three methods on Company19. Everything else happens inside them. Click a stage to see what it does, where it lives in group19.py, what you can tune, and where to look in the run lens.

The rule that decides your pay

Lowest bid wins, but you are paid the second-lowest

Move the sliders. Notice that raising your margin never raises your pay while you are still winning against a rival. It only changes whether you win. The exception is when nobody else bids: then MABLE pays you your own bid.

Where the money goes

Income = revenue − fuel − penalties

Fuel is charged for five ship states

Sailing loaded (laden) or empty (ballast), loading, unloading, and idling. Idle fuel is charged all year whether you win trades or not, so a fleet that never bids still loses money. Your bid prices the extra fuel a trade adds, minus the idle fuel it replaces.

Penalties for undelivered contracts

If you win a trade and fail to deliver it, MABLE fines you roughly what the biggest ship in your fleet would have spent on it. That is why the agent only bids on trades it can fit together, and why receive retries before giving up.

MABLE swallows errors raised inside receive. A crash there does not stop the run. It shows up later as penalties. If the penalty row is not 0, read the log for ran into an exception.
Replacing the schedule of a ship that already has work caused huge fuel spikes in testing, so the agent only gives new trades to free ships. Test carefully if you change this.

    What each trade paid against what it cost you

    Each dot is a trade you bid on. Green: you won and were paid that. Rust: a rival won, and the dot shows the price they were paid, which is the second-lowest bid. Below the grey line means the trade paid less than your cost.

    wonlostbreak-evenyour bid line

    Headroom on the trades you won

    For each won trade: payment ÷ cost − 1. This is the highest margin at which you would still have won it, since you were paid the next bid. Lots of headroom means you could bid higher. Trades where nobody else bid are counted separately: MABLE paid your own bid.
